# Porter’s Five Forces

## Definition
Porter’s Five Forces is a framework for analyzing the competitive intensity of an industry. It helps determine how attractive and profitable a market is.

## Components

### Competitive Rivalry
Intensity of competition among existing firms.

### Threat of New Entrants
How easy it is for new competitors to enter the market.

### Bargaining Power of Suppliers
How much influence suppliers have over pricing and availability.

### Bargaining Power of Buyers
How much influence customers have over pricing and product quality.

### Threat of Substitutes
Availability of alternative products that can replace the current offering.

## When to Use
- Market entry decisions
- Industry analysis
- Pricing and positioning strategy

## How to Apply
1. Evaluate each force individually.
2. Determine which forces are strong or weak.
3. Identify strategic actions to reduce pressure.
4. Use insights to shape competitive strategy.

## Example
In cloud computing, rivalry is high, buyer power is strong, and substitutes are limited — shaping aggressive pricing strategies.

## Limitations
- Provides a static snapshot of a dynamic market.
- Does not account for partnerships or ecosystems.
