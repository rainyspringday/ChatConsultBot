# Lean Canvas

## Definition
Lean Canvas is a startup‑focused adaptation of the Business Model Canvas. It emphasizes problem–solution fit and rapid iteration.

## Components

### Problem
The core customer problems being solved.

### Customer Segments
Target users or customers.

### Unique Value Proposition
Why the solution is compelling and different.

### Solution
The simplest version of the product that solves the problem.

### Channels
How the product reaches customers.

### Revenue Streams
How the business earns money.

### Cost Structure
Key costs required to operate.

### Key Metrics
Indicators that measure progress.

### Unfair Advantage
What competitors cannot easily copy.

## When to Use
- Early‑stage startups
- MVP planning
- Validating assumptions

## How to Apply
1. Identify the core problem.
2. Define the simplest solution.
3. Validate with real users.
4. Track key metrics.
5. Iterate quickly.

## Example
A fintech startup identifies “slow loan approvals” as the problem and proposes an automated scoring engine as the solution.

## Limitations
- Not ideal for mature companies.
- Oversimplifies complex markets.
