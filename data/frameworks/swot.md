# SWOT Analysis

## Definition
SWOT Analysis is a strategic tool used to evaluate a company's internal strengths and weaknesses, as well as external opportunities and threats. It helps identify what the organization does well, where it struggles, and which external factors may influence performance.

## Components

### Strengths
Internal capabilities, assets, or advantages that support success.

### Weaknesses
Internal limitations, gaps, or constraints that hinder performance.

### Opportunities
External trends, market shifts, or conditions that can be leveraged for growth.

### Threats
External risks, competitive pressures, or environmental factors that may negatively impact the business.

## When to Use
- Early‑stage strategy planning
- Competitive assessment
- Product or market evaluation
- Identifying improvement areas

## How to Apply
1. List internal strengths and weaknesses.
2. Identify external opportunities and threats.
3. Prioritize the most impactful items.
4. Develop strategies that leverage strengths and opportunities.
5. Mitigate weaknesses and threats.

## Example
A startup has strong engineering talent (strength) but weak marketing (weakness). Growing demand for AI tools is an opportunity, while large competitors entering the market is a threat.

## Limitations
- Can be subjective without data.
- Does not prioritize items automatically.
- High‑level; requires deeper analysis to act on.
