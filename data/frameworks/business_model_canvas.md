# Business Model Canvas (BMC)

## Definition
The Business Model Canvas is a one‑page visual map describing how a company creates, delivers, and captures value. It helps teams understand and refine their business model.

## Components

### Customer Segments
Groups of people or organizations the business aims to serve.

### Value Proposition
The core value delivered to customers.

### Channels
How the company reaches and communicates with customers.

### Customer Relationships
How the company interacts with customers.

### Revenue Streams
How the business earns money.

### Key Resources
Critical assets required to operate.

### Key Activities
Core tasks needed to deliver the value proposition.

### Key Partnerships
External organizations that support operations.

### Cost Structure
Major costs involved in running the business.

## When to Use
- Designing or refining a business model
- Pitching to investors
- Aligning teams around strategy

## How to Apply
1. Fill each block with concise statements.
2. Validate assumptions with real data.
3. Iterate based on customer feedback.
4. Ensure alignment between blocks.

## Example
A SaaS company defines its value proposition as “automated reporting,” channels as “online sales,” and revenue as “monthly subscription.”

## Limitations
- High‑level; lacks operational detail.
- Assumes stable business environment.
