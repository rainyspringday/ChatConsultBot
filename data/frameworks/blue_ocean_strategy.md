# Blue Ocean Strategy

## Definition
Blue Ocean Strategy focuses on creating uncontested market space by offering unique value while reducing costs. It encourages companies to innovate beyond existing industry boundaries.

## Components (ERRC Grid)

### Eliminate
Factors the industry takes for granted that can be removed.

### Reduce
Elements that can be lowered below industry standards.

### Raise
Elements that should be increased above industry standards.

### Create
New factors that the industry has never offered.

## When to Use
- Entering new markets
- Differentiation strategy
- Innovation planning

## How to Apply
1. Map current industry factors.
2. Identify what to eliminate or reduce.
3. Identify what to raise or create.
4. Build a new value curve.

## Example
Cirque du Soleil eliminated animals (costly), raised artistic quality, and created a new entertainment category.

## Limitations
- High risk.
- Requires strong innovation capability.
