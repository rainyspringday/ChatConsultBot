# McKinsey 7S Framework

## Definition
The McKinsey 7S Framework evaluates organizational alignment across seven internal elements. It helps diagnose gaps that prevent effective execution.

## Components

### Strategy
The plan to achieve competitive advantage.

### Structure
How the organization is arranged.

### Systems
Processes and workflows that support operations.

### Skills
Capabilities and competencies of employees.

### Staff
People and talent within the organization.

### Style
Leadership and management approach.

### Shared Values
Core beliefs that guide behavior.

## When to Use
- Organizational change
- Mergers and acquisitions
- Diagnosing internal misalignment

## How to Apply
1. Assess each of the seven elements.
2. Identify inconsistencies.
3. Develop alignment actions.
4. Monitor progress.

## Example
A company with strong strategy but outdated systems may struggle to execute effectively.

## Limitations
- Time‑consuming.
- Requires deep organizational insight.
